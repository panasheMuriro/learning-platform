# Lab 1: Working with Images

In this lab, you'll explore remote image servers, launch instances from
specific images, manage local cached images, publish a custom image from a
container, and launch a new instance from your custom image.

## Prerequisites

- LXD is installed and initialized (Module 01)
- You understand instance lifecycle (Module 02)

## What you'll do

1. List images on the ubuntu: remote
2. Launch a container from a specific image
3. List locally cached images
4. Publish a custom image from a container
5. Launch a new instance from your custom image
