# Pods and Deployments

## Pods

A **Pod** is the smallest deployable unit in Kubernetes. It encapsulates one or
more containers that share storage and network resources.

## Deployments

A **Deployment** provides declarative updates to Pods and ReplicaSets. You
describe a desired state, and the Deployment Controller changes the actual
state to match it at a controlled rate.
